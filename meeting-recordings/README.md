# Meeting recordings

Recordings, transcripts and notes from team meetings and calls (facility owners, players, mentors, judges).

Naming: `YYYY-MM-DD-topic-summary.md` and `YYYY-MM-DD-topic-transcript.md`. Recordings themselves stay out of git.

## Folders

- `team/`: full-team meetings
- `jake/`: Jake's own calls and notes
- Add a folder per teammate.

A summary says who was there, what we decided, what we learned, and who does what next. Copy anything the team needs to keep into `idea/` or `research/`.
