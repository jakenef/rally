The 48px uppercase action button, in crimson, light or outline.

- Provide `children` (a verb first: "Book a court", "Find a partner"), optional `icon` / `iconAfter` (an `Icon` name), and `href` to render a link.
- `primary` (crimson, `on-crimson` text) once per view, for the thing the page is for. `light` for nav utilities such as Store. `outline` for secondary actions.
- Do not round it into a pill or shrink its height; `radius-btn` and `size-control` are fixed.
