Uppercase sans headlines with one to three accent words swapped into the serif, in `crimson-text`.

- Provide `parts`: strings for sans runs, `{text, serif: true}` for accent words; they are joined with spaces. Or plain `children`.
- `size`: `impact` once per page (manifesto, footer), `h1`/`h2` for titles, `h3` below.
- Accent the verbs and nouns that carry the idea ("DINKING past the kitchen"). Never accent a whole line, never more than three words.
