# Demo

About three minutes on stage. Footage is public YouTube rec play (`clips.md`). Nothing real gets sent: offers show on an on-screen fake phone.

## Scenes

**Scene 1: Chaos vs. Rally** (~1 min, no video). The facility dashboard shows tonight's open play with random courts: a strong player stuck with two beginners, a court fairness score of 41. Press **Rally it**: everyone reshuffles onto balanced courts and scores jump to 90+. Then the money: balanced sessions sell at a premium, and the owner types a pricing rule in plain English.

**Scene 2: Video to court** (~1 min, one YouTube clip). Paste a YouTube link and click one player. Tracking boxes and a court heatmap play over the video (cached), then the player card appears: "3.5 to 4.0, medium confidence · dinker · lives at the kitchen line." Tap **Join Tuesday session** and they land on the right court.

**Scene 3: The cancellation** (~45 s, the ending). Someone cancels 20 minutes before the session. Rally picks the best-fit person on the waitlist, and the fake phone on screen shows: "A spot opened on Court 2 at 6pm, $12." Tap Accept, it's paid in test mode, the court is full, and the dashboard shows "$12 recovered."

**Scene 4: Same rating, different game** (~1 min, two YouTube clips). Two players at the same level, one who hits hard and one who plays soft, shown side by side. Then one court of four equal-rated players. Rating-only puts both hard hitters on one team: fairness 52. Rally mirrors the teams, one of each per side: fairness 91. Line: "DUPR would call both of these courts fair. Players can tell the difference, and Rally can too." Background: `research/play-styles.md`.

**Scene 5: We checked ourselves** (one slide). 6 to 10 YouTube clips with the level in the title, charted as labeled level against Rally's estimated range. Only if the numbers come out decent.

**Bonus: Holy War Night** (~30 s). Courts split Cougars vs. Utes, still balanced on skill, prices moving with the game score.

## Options

- **Option 1, classic:** Scene 1, Scene 2, Scene 3.
- **Option 2, recommended:** Scene 1, Scene 4, Scene 3. It acts out "isn't this just DUPR?" and needs only two good clips.
- **Option 3, safest:** Scene 1, Scene 3, with a screenshot of the video tracking on one slide. Switch to this if the video side isn't working at the midpoint check.
- **Add-ons:** Holy War Night at the end; Scene 5 as one slide.

## Before going on stage

- Clip results pre-processed and cached; the app works with Wi-Fi off.
- The scene 4 court is one click away for "why not just sort by rating?"
- Stripe in test mode.
- Channels credited on screen for every YouTube clip.
