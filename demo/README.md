# Demo

Three minutes on stage. Order: **B, then A, then C**, with D as a 30-second encore if there's time.

## B. "Chaos vs. Rally" (open here, ~60 s)

1. Facility dashboard shows tonight's open play: random rotations, a 4.5 paired with two 2.5s, a court fairness score of 41.
2. Press **Rally it**. Players reshuffle into balanced courts; scores jump to 90+.
3. Money: balanced sessions sell at a premium; the owner types a pricing rule in plain English. Show estimated revenue gained per month.

## A. "Film to court" (~60 s)

1. Upload a teammate's clip (pre-processed; tracking boxes and court heatmap play back).
2. Out comes the player card: "3.5 to 4.0, medium confidence · dinker · lives at the kitchen line."
3. Tap **Join Tuesday Balanced Session, $12**. Cut to the dashboard: they land on the right court.

## C. "The 5:40 cancellation" (~45 s, the ending)

1. A player cancels 20 minutes before the 6pm session.
2. Rally ranks the waitlist by fit; a real phone on stage gets a text: "A 3.5 spot opened on Court 2 at 6pm, $12. Reply YES."
3. They reply, it's paid in test mode, the court is full, the dashboard shows "$12 recovered."

## D. Holy War Night (optional, ~30 s)

Courts grouped Cougars vs. Utes, still balanced on skill, prices moving with the game score.

## Before going on stage

- Clips pre-processed and cached; the app works with Wi-Fi off except the text message.
- The fairness-52-vs-91 comparison is one click away for "why not just sort by rating?"
- Stripe in test mode; the phone that receives the text is a teammate's.
