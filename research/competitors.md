# Competitors and neighbors

Researched 2026-11-06.

## DUPR

- The main pickleball rating system. One number, mostly from match results.
- Partnered with PB Vision in January 2026; since April 2026 a new player can get an initial rating from one recorded match, labeled AI-assisted, first one free. [Source](https://www.dupr.com/post/dupr-partners-with-pb-vision-to-enable-ai-powered-ratings)
- An article says DUPR will use AI video in rating calculations from August 2026. Not confirmed by DUPR. [Source](https://pickleballhq.co/dupr-now-uses-ai-to-rate-your-pickleball-game/)
- No public API found.

## PB Vision

- AI video analytics app. Rates Serve, Return, Offense, Defense, Consistency, Agility.
- A 2026 review says its rating runs 0.3 to 0.7+ above DUPR, inconsistently, with no published accuracy benchmark. [Source](https://fitnessaitrends.com/blog/is-pb-vision-worth-it-serious-pickleball-players-2026/)

## Dinkmate

- "Vision-based rating" from video; first one free. [Site](https://www.dinkmate.ai/)

## Facility booking software (who we plug into)

- **CourtReserve**: web API (HTTPS/JSON: memberships, reservations, events, transactions) on the Advance plan and above, listed at $369/month. Has event and court waitlists with first-come auto-fill. [AI info](https://courtreserve.com/ai-info/), [waitlists](https://intercom.help/courtreserve/en/articles/8925634-create-a-pickleball-waitlist)
- Takeaway: their waitlist fills first-come, not best-fit. That's our backfill pitch.
