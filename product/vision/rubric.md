# Skill rubric for Gemini (spec 010)

Gemini grades one player against these levels. Each level is written as things you can **see** in a short rec-play clip, not as tournament results. Based on the USA Pickleball skill-level descriptions as repeated by [Pickleball Canada](https://pickleballcanada.org/?p=5056), [The Kitchen](https://thekitchenpickle.com/blogs/community/how-to-determine-pickleball-skill-level), [Pickleball Central](https://pickleballcentral.com/blog/the-real-lowdown-on-pickleball-skill-levels) and [Pickleball Australia](https://www.pickleballaus.org/rating-ability-guide). Approximate: we couldn't reach the official text. The AI lane owns this file; edit it freely.

## 2.5
- Rallies are short; many points end on the serve, return or third shot.
- Frequent unforced errors: balls into the net or long, mishits.
- Mostly stays back near the baseline; rarely gets to the kitchen line.
- Dinks, when tried, don't last more than two or three shots.

## 3.0
- Can rally at a medium pace, but can't control it: soft and hard shots come out at the wrong times.
- Gets to the kitchen line sometimes, often late or caught in the transition zone.
- Third shots are mostly drives or pop-ups; drops land high or in the net.
- Some consistency on serves and returns; volleys are often blocked back high.

## 3.5
- Moves to the kitchen line after the return most of the time.
- Attempts third-shot drops, landing some of them.
- Can sustain short dink rallies (around 5 shots), but speeds up balls that are too low.
- Starting to work with a partner: moving together, covering the middle.

## 4.0
- Sustains dink rallies of 10 to 15 shots with few errors.
- Drops most third shots into the kitchen; chooses between drop and drive on purpose.
- Resets hard balls softly into the kitchen instead of hitting back hard.
- Few unforced errors; shapes points to attack a weaker opponent.

## 4.5
- Patient and consistent at the line; rarely makes an unforced error.
- Speeds up only on balls that are high enough, and counters speed-ups well.
- Covers for the partner, moves as a unit, may stack.
- Wins the transition: gets from baseline to kitchen line cleanly under pressure.

## 5.0 and up
- Dink rallies of 20 to 30 shots, with deliberate placement and disguise.
- Almost no unforced errors; every shot has a clear purpose.
- Fast, efficient footwork; always in ready position.
- Attacks and resets equally well; controls the pace of the whole rally.

## How to grade

- Grade only the player described. Use the others only as context (a 4.0 looks better against 3.0s).
- Give a range half a point wide (for example 3.5 to 4.0), or a full point wide if the clip is short or the player is hard to see.
- Base the range on behaviors you actually saw, and list them as reasons.
